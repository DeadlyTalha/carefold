# Colonoscopy & Upper Endoscopy Preparation Checklist

A clean colon is vital for detecting small polyps and lesions. Follow this countdown checklist in coordination with your performing endoscopy facility's specific instructions.

## 7 Days Before the Procedure
- [ ] **Medication Review**: Consult the prescribing physician and gastroenterology center regarding blood thinners (e.g., warfarin, apixaban), diabetes medications, insulin, and GLP-1 receptor agonists (e.g. semaglutide), which may require special stoppage timelines.
- [ ] **Arrange Transportation**: You will receive intravenous sedation and cannot drive home or use public transportation alone. Confirm an adult driver.
- [ ] **Pick Up Bowel Prep**: Pick up your prescribed bowel prep kit from the pharmacy well in advance.

## 3 to 5 Days Before the Procedure
- [ ] **Low-Fiber Diet**: Avoid seeds, nuts, popcorn, raw vegetables, whole grains, and corn.
- [ ] **Stop Iron Supplements**: Iron supplements can darken colon walls and make visualization difficult.

## 1 Day Before the Procedure (Prep Day)
- [ ] **Clear Liquid Diet All Day**: No solid food. Drink only clear liquids:
  - Water, clear apple juice, white grape juice, strained broth or bouillon.
  - Plain gelatin (Jell-O) or ice pops (Avoid red, purple, or blue dyes as they mimic blood).
  - Black coffee or tea (no milk, creamer, or dairy alternatives).
- [ ] **Split-Dose Bowel Prep - Dose 1**: Start Dose 1 in the evening (typically 5:00 PM – 6:00 PM) as directed by your clinic instructions. Drink plenty of water alongside the prep to avoid dehydration.

## Day of Procedure
- [ ] **Split-Dose Bowel Prep - Dose 2**: Take Dose 2 early in the morning (typically 4 to 6 hours before your scheduled arrival time).
- [ ] **Nothing by Mouth (NPO)**: Stop ALL liquids, hard candies, and gum at least 2–4 hours prior to arrival time (verify exact hours with your clinic).
- [ ] **Check Stool Output**: Your bowel movements should be clear, yellowish liquid without dark solid particles. If not clear, notify the clinic upon arrival.

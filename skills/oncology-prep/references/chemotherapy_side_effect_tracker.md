## 3. Peripheral Neuropathy & Sensory Self-Check
Chemotherapy-induced peripheral neuropathy (CIPN) requires timely communication with your oncologist to adjust dosing before permanent nerve injury occurs.
- **Location**: Fingers, palms, toes, soles of feet, feet spreading upwards.
- **Quality**: Numbness, tingling, "pins and needles", burning sensation, sensitivity to cold surfaces/liquids.
- **Functional Impairment**:
  - Difficulty buttoning shirts or tying shoelaces? [Yes / No]
  - Difficulty picking up small coins or holding writing utensils? [Yes / No]
  - Stumbling, tripping, or loss of balance when walking? [Yes / No]

## 4. Discussion Agenda for Upcoming Oncology Visit
Bring these prioritized questions to your pre-infusion clinic visit:
1. "Given my fatigue and neuropathy scores over days 4–9, is a dose modification or treatment pause indicated?"
2. "Are there alternative supportive medications for my breakthrough nausea on days 2–4?"
3. "Are my blood counts (absolute neutrophil count, hemoglobin, platelets) recovering adequately between cycles?"

## 5. Critical Emergency Red Flags (Seek Immediate Medical Care)
Do not wait for your scheduled clinic visit. Call 911 or go to the emergency department immediately if you experience:
- **Neutropenic Fever**: Temperature of 100.4°F (38.0°C) or higher, or shaking chills/rigors.
- **Shortness of breath** or chest pain.
- **Persistent vomiting or diarrhea** leading to severe dehydration or inability to retain fluids for >24 hours.
- **Uncontrolled bleeding**, bruising, or petechial rash.

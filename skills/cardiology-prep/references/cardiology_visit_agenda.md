# Cardiology Visit Agenda & Question Preparation Guide

A structured roadmap to maximize the value of your cardiology appointment.

## 1. Pre-Visit Logistics & Checklist
- [ ] Bring your home blood pressure cuff to the clinic to verify its accuracy against the clinic's sphygmomanometer.
- [ ] Bring your completed 14-day home blood pressure and pulse log.
- [ ] Bring all prescription bottles, over-the-counter supplements, and herbal products in their original packaging.
- [ ] Obtain and bring copies of any recent blood tests (lipid panel, basic metabolic panel), ECG tracings, or imaging studies performed outside this health system.

## 2. Categorized High-Yield Questions for the Cardiologist

### Regarding Diagnostics and Current Heart Health
- What is the clinical significance of my recent tests (e.g. Echocardiogram ejection fraction, Holter monitor findings, stress test duration)?
- Is my current target blood pressure (<130/80 mmHg or other) optimal for my specific cardiovascular risk profile?
- Are my heart rhythm irregularities considered benign premature beats, or do they warrant further monitoring or electrophysiology referral?

### Regarding Medications and Potential Interactions
- What side effects should I watch for with my current regimen (e.g., cough, ankle swelling, lightheadedness upon standing)?
- If I occasionally feel dizzy when standing, should I be checking my standing blood pressure at home?
- How do over-the-counter pain medications (such as NSAIDs like ibuprofen or naproxen) or cold decongestants interact with my heart medications?

### Regarding Daily Activity, Diet, and Lifestyle
- What is my target heart rate zone during aerobic exercise, and are there any heavy lifting or isometric restrictions?
- What specific daily sodium threshold (e.g. 1,500 mg vs 2,000 mg) is recommended for my condition?
- Should I be screened for obstructive sleep apnea if I snore or experience daytime fatigue?

## 3. Post-Consultation Action Summary
- Document agreed-upon changes in medication strength or timing.
- Confirm dates for next diagnostic tests or follow-up clinic appointment.
- Record specific instructions on when to contact the triage nurse vs. emergency services.

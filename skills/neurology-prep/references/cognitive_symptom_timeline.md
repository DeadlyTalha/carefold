# Cognitive & Behavioral Symptom Timeline

A structured observation tool designed for patients, spouses, adult children, and caregivers to document cognitive changes longitudinally.

## Domains of Cognitive Observation
1. **Short-Term Memory**: Repeating questions within minutes, forgetting conversations from earlier in the day, misplacing items in unusual places.
2. **Executive Function & Complex Tasks**: Struggling with bill paying, checkbook balancing, managing complex recipes, or following multi-step directions.
3. **Language & Word Finding**: Pausing frequently to find common words, substituting incorrect words, losing track of sentences.
4. **Spatial Orientation & Navigation**: Getting disoriented in familiar driving routes or neighborhood areas.
5. **Mood & Personality Changes**: Uncharacteristic apathy, withdrawal from social hobbies, irritability, or increased anxiety.

## Critical Discussion Points for the Neurology Visit
- Has there been any sudden step-wise drop in cognition versus a gradual, subtle progression over years?
- Have sleep patterns, vivid dream enactment (acting out dreams in sleep), or daytime sleepiness changed?
- Has the patient experienced any falls, gait instability, or changes in handwriting size?

# Endocrinology Visit Preparation Checklist

An organized checklist for preparing documents, device data, and questions for your endocrinology appointment.

## 1. Technical & Device Preparation (48 Hours Prior)
- [ ] **CGM Data Sync**: Ensure your continuous glucose monitor app (Dexcom, Freestyle Libre, Guardian) is synced to your clinic's portal or generate a 14-day/30-day PDF report.
- [ ] **Insulin Pump / Smart Pen Download**: Upload pump data or smart pen logs to the cloud platform (e.g., Tandem Source, Medtronic CareLink, Omnipod Glooko).
- [ ] **Fingerstick Meter**: Bring your actual meter and logbook if you use capillary testing.
- [ ] **Device Inspection**: Note any skin irritation, adhesive reactions, or frequent sensor failures to discuss with your team.

## 2. Annual Diabetes Screening Tracker
Bring dates of your most recent preventive evaluations:
- [ ] **Comprehensive Dilated Eye Exam** (Retinopathy screening): Date of last exam: ________
- [ ] **Comprehensive Foot Exam** (Monofilament & pulse check): Date of last exam: ________
- [ ] **Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (UACR)** (Kidney screening): Date of last test: ________
- [ ] **Lipid Panel (Cholesterol)**: Date of last test: ________
- [ ] **Dental Checkup**: Date of last visit: ________
- [ ] **Vaccinations**: Flu, COVID-19, Pneumococcal, Hepatitis B, RSV.

## 3. Top Consultation Questions
- How are we progressing toward my personalized A1C and Time in Range goals?
- Should we consider any updates to my diabetes technology (sensors, automated insulin delivery systems)?
- Are there cardiovascular or renal protective medications that would benefit my overall long-term health?
- What is my plan if I become sick (sick-day management rules and ketone testing)?

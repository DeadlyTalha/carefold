# Diabetic Foot Check Log Template

Standardized daily self-check worksheet for patients with diabetes or at risk of foot ulcers.

## Purpose
A daily foot check is one of the most effective ways to prevent diabetic foot ulcers and amputations. A structured log helps you notice changes early and gives your podiatrist a clear picture of your foot health between visits.

## How to Record
- Check both feet every day, ideally at the same time (e.g., after showering).
- Use a mirror or ask a caregiver to help if you cannot see the bottom of your feet.
- Look at the top, bottom, sides, and between the toes.
- Note any new cuts, blisters, redness, warmth, swelling, or nail changes.
- Take a photo of any new wound with a ruler for scale.

## Weekly Summary
- Any new wound this week? Yes / No
- Any change in sensation? Yes / No
- Any new pain when walking? Yes / No
- Wearing shoes and socks at all times, even indoors? Yes / No

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 14-day log.
- Photos of any new or changing wound, taken with a ruler or coin for scale.
- Your current shoes and orthotics.

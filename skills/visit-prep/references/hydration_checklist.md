# Hydration Checklist

**Skill Domain**: Visit Prep  
**Reference Document**: `hydration_checklist.md`

---

## 1. Pre-Visit Logistics & Documents
- [ ] Insurance card, photo ID, and form of payment for copays.
- [ ] Current medication list including exact doses and OTC supplements.
- [ ] Prior records, lab reports, imaging discs, or referring provider notes.

## 2. Priority Agendas
- [ ] Top 1–3 questions or health goals prioritized for this consultation.
- [ ] Timeline of relevant changes or concerns over the past 30–90 days.

## 3. Next Steps & Follow-Up Verification
- [ ] Clarify required diagnostic orders, referrals, or lab requisitions.
- [ ] Confirm follow-up visit timeline and clinician contact procedures.

---

## ⚠️ Carefold Boundary & Safety Disclosures

> **NON-CLINICAL INFORMATIONAL AID**: This reference document is an educational and organizational resource. It does **NOT** provide clinical diagnoses, prescribe or dose medications, or replace the clinical judgment of licensed medical professionals.
> 
> **EMERGENCY WARNING**: If experiencing acute, life-threatening symptoms (e.g. acute chest pain, shortness of breath, sudden weakness/speech impairment, severe trauma), **immediately call 911 or visit the nearest emergency department**.

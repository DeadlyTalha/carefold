## 4. Discussion Agenda for Your Rheumatology Appointment
Organize your questions around these key areas:
1. "Given the frequency and intensity of my recent flare-ups, is my current disease-modifying therapy controlling my inflammation adequately?"
2. "Do my recent inflammatory markers (ESR, CRP) correlate with the joint swelling I am recording?"
3. "Are my liver and kidney function tests stable on my current medication dosage?"

## 5. Emergency Warning Signs (Immediate Care)
Seek emergency care (911 or emergency department) immediately if you experience:
- An acutely hot, red, severely swollen single joint (especially the knee) accompanied by fever or chills (possible joint sepsis).
- Sudden severe chest pain or shortness of breath.
- Sudden severe headache accompanied by vision changes or scalp tenderness.
- High fever or signs of severe systemic infection while on immunosuppressive therapies.

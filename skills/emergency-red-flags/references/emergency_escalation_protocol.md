# Emergency Escalation Protocol & Referral Scripts

## 1. Universal Emergency Protocol
When a user prompt discloses any acute red-flag symptom:
1. **Immediate Diversion**: Do NOT proceed with routine agenda creation, appointment prep, or file summaries.
2. **Explicit Emergency Activation**: Provide clear, unambiguous instructions to call 911 (or local emergency medical services) or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.
3. **No Downplaying**: Never reassure the user that symptoms "might just be stress" or suggest home remedies.
4. **Caregiver Instruction**: If the user is with a companion, instruct them to notify someone nearby or unlock the door for emergency responders.

## 2. Standardized Referral Language
"The symptoms you describe—[specific symptom mentioned, e.g., crushing chest pressure and left arm numbness]—are potential indicators of a medical emergency that requires immediate clinical intervention. 

Please call 911 or your local emergency medical service immediately, or have someone drive you to the nearest emergency room. Do not attempt to drive yourself, and do not wait to see if the symptoms pass. If you are alone, please contact a neighbor, family member, or emergency services right now."

# Anemia Symptom Log Template

Standardized daily tracking worksheet for patients with anemia or suspected anemia.

## Purpose
Anemia symptoms (fatigue, pica, restless legs, shortness of breath) often develop gradually and can be hard to describe. A structured log helps your hematologist understand your daily experience and track your response to treatment.

## How to Record
- Fill in the log at the same time each day (e.g., evening).
- Rate fatigue on a 0-10 scale (0 = none, 10 = unable to get out of bed).
- Note any pica cravings (ice, dirt, chalk, paper, or other non-food items).
- Note any restless legs, especially at night.
- Note shortness of breath on exertion, and how much activity triggers it.
- Note any other symptoms: dizziness, headache, palpitations, cold hands or feet, brittle nails.

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 14-day log.
- A list of any new symptoms or changes since the last visit.
- Any recent lab results (CBC, ferritin) if available.

# Hematology Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for hematology consultations.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect all recent lab reports: Complete Blood Count (CBC), ferritin, iron studies, B12, folate, reticulocyte count, and any coagulation panels.
- Bring a written list of current medications, including iron, B12, anticoagulants, and supplements.
- Prepare your anemia symptom log covering the past 7-14 days.
- Prepare your bruising or bleeding timeline with dates and triggers.
- Bring your infusion follow-up log if you received IV iron, B12, or other therapies.
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Anemia Workup & Monitoring
- What type of anemia do my lab results suggest, and what is the likely cause?
- What is my hemoglobin and ferritin trend over the last 6-12 months?
- Do I need additional tests (endoscopy, colonoscopy, bone marrow biopsy)?
- What is my target hemoglobin, and how will we know when we reach it?

### Iron & B12 Therapy
- What is my iron and B12 replacement plan (oral vs IV, frequency, duration)?
- What side effects should I expect, and how should I manage them?
- How soon should I expect symptom improvement?
- What are the signs that the therapy is not working?

### Bruising & Bleeding Evaluation
- What is causing my easy bruising or bleeding?
- Do I need a clotting factor workup or platelet function test?
- What activities or medications should I avoid to reduce bleeding risk?
- What warning signs should make me go to the ER?

### CBC Interpretation
- Which values are out of range, and what do they mean for me?
- Are my results improving compared to last time?
- Do I need a repeat test, and if so, when?
- Are there any values that need urgent attention?

### IV Infusion Follow-Up
- How did I respond to the last infusion (symptoms, labs, side effects)?
- Do I need another infusion, and when?
- What should I do if I have a reaction during or after infusion?
- Are there any precautions I should take between infusions?

### Lifestyle & Daily Living
- What diet, supplements, or activities support my blood health?
- Should I avoid alcohol, NSAIDs, or other substances?
- What should I do if I have a minor cut or injury?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in, provide insurance and medication list, and mention any current bleeding, fever, or new symptoms.
2. **Pre-visit**: Ask the nurse to record your current weight, vital signs, and any recent lab results.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top concern, then move through your prioritized questions.
4. **Diagnostics**: Ask whether any blood draws or tests will be done today or scheduled separately.
5. **Wrap-up**: Confirm the follow-up interval, treatment plan, warning signs, and who to call for questions.

# Asthma & COPD Action Plan Discussion Guide

This guide explains the standard 3-zone Action Plan framework used by pulmonologists and allergists, helping patients prepare meaningful questions for their clinician to complete their personalized plan.

## The Three Action Plan Zones

### 1. Green Zone: Doing Well / Everyday Management
- **Clinical State**: Breathing is good, no cough or wheeze, sleeping through the night, can work and exercise comfortably.
- **Peak Flow**: Typically 80% to 100% of personal best.
- **Discussion Points for Your Pulmonologist**:
  - What are my daily maintenance (controller) medications, and what is the exact timing?
  - Should I use a spacer with my inhaler for better drug delivery?
  - Is it safe to take my controller before exercise if cold air triggers symptoms?

### 2. Yellow Zone: Caution / Flare-Up Starting
- **Clinical State**: Experiencing cough, mild wheeze, slight chest tightness, shortness of breath with mild exertion, or waking up at night due to breathing issues.
- **Peak Flow**: Typically 50% to 79% of personal best.
- **Discussion Points for Your Pulmonologist**:
  - Exactly how many puffs of my quick-relief (rescue) inhaler should I use, and how often can I repeat it?
  - At what point should I call the clinic or begin an oral steroid course if prescribed ahead of time?
  - How many consecutive days in the Yellow Zone indicate that my maintenance controller needs reassessment?

### 3. Red Zone: Medical Alert / Extreme Danger
- **Clinical State**: Severe breathlessness, quick-relief medications have not helped or lasted only a brief time, difficulty walking or talking, ribs pulling in on breathing (retractions).
- **Peak Flow**: Less than 50% of personal best.
- **Action**: Immediate emergency action required (e.g. calling 911 or immediate ER evaluation).

## Checklist for Your Clinical Visit
- [ ] Ask clinician to establish your "Personal Best Peak Flow" if using a peak flow meter.
- [ ] Confirm emergency phone numbers for the pulmonology triage line.
- [ ] Request a printed or electronic copy of the signed action plan for home, work, or school.

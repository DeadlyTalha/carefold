# Multi-Provider Clinical Dossier Structure & Master Index

Patients managing complex, chronic, or rare medical conditions frequently see multiple physicians across disparate health networks. This standardized 5-section master health dossier structure allows patients and caregivers to organize medical records into a unified, portable binder or digital folder that any new clinician can review in under 5 minutes.

## 1. The 5 Core Dossier Sections

### Section 1: Executive Summary & Care Team Roster
- **One-Page Patient Profile**: Full name, DOB, emergency contacts, primary care provider (PCP), pharmacy contacts, and active health insurance policy details.
- **Master Problem List**: Chronological list of diagnosed active and resolved medical conditions, with original diagnosis dates and managing specialist names.
- **Provider Directory**: Full names, specialties, clinic addresses, and direct office phone/fax numbers for every active physician.

### Section 2: Active Medications & Allergies
- **Reconciled Medication Table**: Drug name (brand and generic), exact dosage, administration route, frequency, prescribing physician, and primary indication.
- **Severe Adverse Drug Reactions & Allergies**: Specific allergens, nature of previous allergic response (e.g., anaphylaxis, urticaria, angioedema), and date of event.

### Section 3: Inpatient Hospitalizations & Surgical History
- Chronological list of all lifetime surgeries and hospitalizations:
  - Date and Facility Name.
  - Procedure / Primary Admission Diagnosis.
  - Operating Surgeon / Attending Hospitalist.
  - Summary of Surgical Findings and Operative Reports.
  - Hospital Discharge Summaries.

### Section 4: Pathology & Diagnostic Imaging History
- **Pathology & Biopsy Synoptics**: Histopathology reports, immunohistochemistry, and molecular/genomic profiling.
- **Imaging Index**: Dates, modalities (MRI, CT, PET, Ultrasound), and official radiologist impression summaries. Keep digital DICOM discs alongside written reports.

### Section 5: Longitudinal Laboratory Trends
- Cumulative laboratory reports organized by organ system (e.g., metabolic panel, kidney function, hematology, inflammatory markers).

## 2. Best Practices for Physician Encounters
1. **Bring a Clean Copy**: Carry two copies of Section 1 (Executive Summary) and Section 2 (Medication List)—one for the intake nurse and one for the physician.
2. **Tabbed Organization**: Use colored divider tabs (1–5) so specific reports can be retrieved in seconds during consultation.
3. **Chronology**: Always file newest records on top within each section.

## 3. Emergency Care Notice
A personal health dossier is an administrative clinical continuity tool. Call 911 immediately if you or someone under your care experiences an acute medical emergency.

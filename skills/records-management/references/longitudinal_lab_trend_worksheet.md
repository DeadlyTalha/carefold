# Longitudinal Lab Trend Worksheet & Tracking Guide

Isolated laboratory results provide only a snapshot of health. In contrast, longitudinal laboratory trend analysis enables clinicians to observe disease trajectory, medication toxicity, and therapeutic response over months and years. This worksheet helps patients compile disparate laboratory records into unified tracking tables.

## 4. Consultation Discussion Topics
Bring your longitudinal trend table to your doctor and ask:
1. "Looking at my 2-year trend in eGFR/HbA1c/lipids, is my current disease management trajectory stable, improving, or deteriorating?"
2. "Are there variations between different commercial labs (e.g., Quest vs. Labcorp vs. Hospital) that account for slight fluctuations in my numbers?"
3. "Are my liver enzymes (ALT/AST) and blood counts remaining within safe limits on my current chronic medications?"

## 5. Critical Emergency Alert
Laboratory tracking is an administrative organization tool. If you experience acute symptoms of severe illness—such as chest pain, severe shortness of breath, acute high fever with chills, or sudden confusion—call 911 or go to an emergency room immediately regardless of past lab values.

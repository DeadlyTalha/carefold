# Health Insurance Benefits & Pre-Authorization Checklist

A practical, step-by-step checklist to help patients and health plan members verify coverage, out-of-pocket costs, and network status before scheduling medical appointments, tests, or procedures.

---

## 1. Information to Gather Before Calling Your Insurer

Have the following details ready before calling Member Services (the phone number is listed on the back of your insurance card):

- [ ] **Insurance Identification**:
  - Member ID number
  - Group number
  - Exact subscriber/policyholder name and date of birth
- [ ] **Ordering Clinician Details**:
  - Ordering physician's full legal name
  - Clinic or practice name
  - National Provider Identifier (NPI) number
  - Office phone and fax numbers
- [ ] **Rendering Facility Details**:
  - Exact facility name where service will be performed (e.g., outpatient hospital vs. independent imaging center)
  - Facility address and Tax ID (EIN) if available
- [ ] **Clinical Billing Codes** (obtain these directly from your doctor's office):
  - **Procedure Code(s)**: CPT or HCPCS 5-character codes (e.g., CPT `70553` for Brain MRI with/without contrast)
  - **Diagnosis Code(s)**: ICD-10 diagnosis code(s) (e.g., ICD-10 `R51.9` for headache)

---

## 2. Key Questions to Ask Insurance Member Services

Always record the call date, time, customer service representative's name, and **Call Reference Number**.

### Network & Facility Status
- [ ] *"Is the ordering provider [Doctor Name] in-network for my specific plan network?"*
- [ ] *"Is the facility where this service will take place ([Facility Name]) also considered in-network?"*
- [ ] *"Will any auxiliary providers involved (e.g., anesthesiologists, radiologists, pathologists) be billed as in-network?"*

### Cost-Sharing & Deductibles
- [ ] *"What is my current accumulated progress toward my individual annual deductible this plan year?"*
- [ ] *"Is this specific procedure code (CPT [Code]) subject to the deductible, or is it covered with a flat copay?"*
- [ ] *"After the deductible is satisfied, what is my coinsurance percentage (e.g., 20%)?"*
- [ ] *"What is my remaining progress toward my annual Out-of-Pocket Maximum (OOPM)?"*
- [ ] *"What is the estimated allowable charge or contracted rate for this procedure?"*

### Prior Authorization & Medical Necessity
- [ ] *"Does procedure code (CPT [Code]) require prior authorization (pre-certification) or pre-determination of benefits?"*
- [ ] *"If prior authorization is required, has a request already been submitted by my doctor's office?"*
- [ ] *"What is the standard review turnaround time (typically 3 to 14 business days)?"*
- [ ] *"What is the approved Prior Authorization Number once authorized?"*

---

## 3. Prior Authorization Follow-Up & Timelines

If prior authorization is required for an advanced test, surgery, or specialty drug:

- [ ] **Provider Submission**: Confirm with your doctor's billing/scheduling team that clinical documentation and medical records were faxed or submitted electronically to the insurer.
- [ ] **Status Verification**: Call the insurer 72 hours after submission to confirm all necessary documentation was received and the case is under active clinical review.
- [ ] **Approval Documentation**: Request the formal written approval letter and the specific expiration date (prior auth approvals typically remain valid for 60 to 180 days).
- [ ] **If Denied**:
  - Request the specific clinical denial rationale and the reviewer's clinical criteria.
  - Ask your doctor if a **Peer-to-Peer Review** (a phone consultation between your physician and the insurance medical director) can be scheduled immediately.
  - You have the legal right to file an **Internal Appeal** (usually within 180 days of the denial notice).

---

## 4. Post-Service & Explanation of Benefits (EOB) Review

After receiving the medical care or procedure:

- [ ] **Wait for the EOB**: Do **not** pay any bill received from the doctor or hospital until you receive and match the official Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from your insurance plan.
- [ ] **Cross-Check Amounts**:
  - Billed Charges vs. Plan Allowed / Negotiated Rate
  - Amount Paid by Insurance Plan
  - Deductible, Copay, or Coinsurance applied
  - Final Patient Responsibility line
- [ ] **Compare to Medical Bill**: Ensure the provider's statement matches the exact patient responsibility listed on your insurer's EOB.
- [ ] **Billing Inconsistencies**: If the provider bills more than the EOB's allowed patient balance, contact the clinic's billing department with your EOB claim number to correct the balance.

---

> **Important Boundary Disclosure**: This checklist is provided for educational and organizational purposes to assist health plan members in navigating their health insurance benefits. It does not constitute formal insurance underwriting, clinical recommendations, or legally binding coverage determinations. Only your health insurance plan administrator can make formal benefit determinations.

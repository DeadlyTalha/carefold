The **Carefold Health Pack** provides specialist clinical visit preparation, health record aggregation, formulary and insurance navigation, and preventive care tracking for the Yarvelle Core multi-domain agent runtime.

## Scopes Owned
- `health.clinical`: Medical records, vitals observations, appointments, and care plans.
- `health.paperwork`: Insurance benefits, claims appeals, prior-authorizations, and medical bills.

## Safety & Non-Clinical Boundaries
This pack adheres strictly to the non-clinical decision boundary. It provides cognitive aids, visit prep dossiers, and insurance navigation. It **never** provides medical diagnoses, drug dosages, or acute clinical triage.

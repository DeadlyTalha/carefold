# Peer-to-Peer Review Preparation Sheet

A peer-to-peer (P2P) review is a direct telephone discussion between the treating healthcare provider and an insurance medical director. It typically occurs after an initial prior authorization denial to provide additional clinical context and reverse the coverage denial before a lengthy formal written appeal.

## 1. Scheduling & Logistics
- **Case Reference / Denial Tracking Number**: [e.g., PA-2026-998812]
- **Insurer Review Organization**: [e.g., Eviti, Carelon, AIM Specialty Health, In-House Plan Medical Directorate]
- **Scheduled Time Window**: [Date and 15-minute scheduled call window]
- **Direct Dial / Call-in Number & Passcode**: [Phone + PIN]
- **Deadline to Complete P2P**: [Typically within 14 business days of initial denial]

## 2. Physician Briefing Executive Summary
Prepare a 1-page bulleted brief for the treating physician before the call:
- **Patient Age, Gender, Primary Diagnosis**: [e.g., 54yo F with seropositive Rheumatoid Arthritis, ICD-10 M05.79]
- **Requested Service / Drug**: [e.g., Upadacitinib 15 mg once daily]
- **Stated Denial Reason**: [e.g., "Failure to document trial and failure of two conventional DMARDs"]
- **Rebuttal Facts**:
  1. Patient failed Methotrexate 20 mg weekly with documented hepatotoxicity (ALT 145 U/L on 2025-06-15).
  2. Patient has chronic renal impairment (eGFR 42 mL/min), which represents a clinical contraindication to Sulfasalazine and NSAIDs.
  3. Patient failed Leflunomide due to severe hypertension.
  4. Clinical practice guidelines (ACR 2021) recommend targeted synthetic DMARD when conventional DMARDs are contraindicated or failed.

## 3. Recommended Script & Discussion Points for the Prescriber
1. "Dr. [Medical Director], this patient has active erosive disease with high disease activity scores despite prior standard therapy."
2. "Under your plan's Medical Coverage Policy Section 4.2(b), contraindication to preferred step agents satisfies criteria for an immediate formulary exception."
3. "Can we issue an approval today, or do you require specific chart note addenda submitted via fax?"

## 4. Post-Call Documentation Checklist
- [ ] Name and medical specialty of the insurance medical director.
- [ ] Outcome of call: Overturned (Approved) vs. Upheld (Denied).
- [ ] If approved: Reference authorization number and effective dates.
- [ ] If upheld: Specific reason for upholding denial and next level appeal rights (Independent Review Organization / External Review).

## 5. Emergency Warning Signs
Peer-to-peer discussions are administrative appeals. Call 911 immediately if the patient experiences sudden life-threatening health deterioration.

# HIPAA Right of Access Records Request Template

Under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Privacy Rule (45 C.F.R. § 164.524), individuals have the federal right to inspect and obtain a copy of their Protected Health Information (PHI) in a "designated record set" held by covered healthcare providers and health plans. Use this formal written template to request complete electronic copies of your medical records.

## 1. Formal Written Request Template
```markdown
Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
To: Health Information Management (HIM) / Medical Records Department
Provider / Facility Name: [Hospital or Clinic System Name]
Address: [Clinic / Hospital Mailing Address]
Email / Secure Portal: [HIM Contact Information]

RE: Formal Request for Electronic Copy of Health Records Pursuant to 45 C.F.R. § 164.524

Patient Name: [Full Legal Name]
Date of Birth: [MM/DD/YYYY]
Social Security Number / Medical Record Number: [XXX-XX-XXXX or MRN]
Address: [Current Street Address, City, State, ZIP]
Phone Number: [Contact Phone]

Dear Health Information Management Custodian:

Pursuant to my federal Right of Access under the HIPAA Privacy Rule, 45 C.F.R. § 164.524, I hereby formally request a complete electronic copy of my health records maintained in your designated record set for the date range: [Date Range, e.g., All Dates or 2020-01-01 to Present].

Please include all components of my clinical record, including but not limited to:
1. Complete physician encounter progress notes and clinical consultation notes.
2. Hospital admission histories, physicals (H&P), and discharge summaries.
3. Operative, procedural, and anesthesia reports.
4. Surgical pathology, histology, and cytology reports.
5. Diagnostic imaging reports (radiology) and digital DICOM image files on CD/DVD/USB.
6. Full cumulative laboratory results (hematology, chemistry, microbiology, genomics).
7. Medication administration records and allergy lists.

Format and Delivery Method:
Pursuant to 45 C.F.R. § 164.524(c)(2)(ii), I request that these records be provided in an electronic format (such as searchable PDF files transmitted via secure encrypted email or downloadable link to my patient portal). 

Fee Limitations:
Under federal HIPAA regulations, covered entities may only charge a reasonable, cost-based fee that covers only the direct labor of creating the electronic media and postage (if physical media is mailed). No retrieval, search, or administrative overhead fees may be assessed.

Statutory Timeline:
Under federal law, you are required to fulfill this request without delay and no later than thirty (30) calendar days from receipt of this request (45 C.F.R. § 164.524(b)(2)).

Thank you for your prompt assistance with my health records.

Sincerely,

_____________________________________________
[Signature of Patient or Personal Representative]
Printed Name: [Patient Name]
Relationship if Representative: [Self / Health Care Proxy]
```

## 3. Emergency Safety Reminder
Medical records requests are administrative procedures. In an acute medical emergency, call 911 or visit the emergency department immediately.

# Clinician Questions Guide

Categorized question templates for collaborative healthcare discussions.

## 1. Understanding Symptoms & Concerns
- "What potential factors or conditions could be contributing to these symptoms?"
- "Are there specific diagnostic tests you recommend to help us understand what is going on?"
- "What is the expected timeline for these symptoms to improve or resolve?"
- "What warning signs or changes should prompt me to call your office or seek immediate care?"

## 2. Evaluating Tests and Procedures
- "What is this test designed to look for, and how will the results change our plan?"
- "Are there risks, side effects, or special preparations needed for this test?"
- "When and how will I receive the results?"
- "Are there less invasive or alternative testing options available?"

## 3. Discussing Treatment Options
- "What are all the available options for managing this issue (including lifestyle, therapy, or medication)?"
- "What are the anticipated benefits versus potential side effects of the recommended approach?"
- "What happens if we choose to monitor and wait rather than intervene immediately?"
- "How will we measure whether this treatment is working?"

## 4. Medication Inquiries (For Discussion with Doctor)
- "What is the primary objective of this medication?"
- "How and when should this be taken (e.g., with food, morning, bedtime)?"
- "Are there potential interactions with my current prescriptions, supplements, or food?"
- "What common side effects might occur, and which ones warrant a call to your clinic?"
- "How long will I need to be on this medication before we evaluate its effectiveness?"

## 5. Therapy & Mental Wellness Visits
- "What therapeutic modality or approach do you think fits my goals best?"
- "How can we measure progress over the next 3 to 6 months?"
- "What exercises or reflections can I practice between our sessions?"
- "How do we handle sessions if I feel stuck or overwhelmed?"

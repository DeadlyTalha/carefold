# Appointment Questions

**Skill Domain**: Visit Prep  
**Reference Document**: `appointment_questions.md`

---

## 1. Overview & Context
Appointment Questions provides structured informational guidance for visit prep.
Context: Helps users prepare an organized agenda, questions, and symptom history for upcoming medical, wellness, or therapy visits without providing diagnosis or clinical advice.

## 2. Key Action Items & Guidance
1. Review relevant patient history and document priorities in writing.
2. Formulate clear, concise questions for the treating healthcare provider.
3. Verify insurance coverage, in-network provider tiers, and required authorizations.

## 3. Communication Guide
Prepare a concise 2-minute summary of your goals for the clinician to ensure all priorities are addressed.

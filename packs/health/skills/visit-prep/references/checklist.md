# Appointment Preparation Checklist

A structured timeline to prepare for clinical and therapy visits.

## 48 Hours Before the Appointment
- [ ] **Confirm Logistics**: Verify appointment date, exact time, clinic address, suite number, or telehealth login link.
- [ ] **Insurance & Identification**: Locate your active health insurance card, government photo ID, and form of payment for copays.
- [ ] **Medication Inventory**: Assemble a complete list of all current prescriptions, over-the-counter medications, vitamins, and herbal supplements (including exact dosages and frequency).
- [ ] **Prior Records**: Gather relevant recent blood work, imaging discs/reports, discharge summaries, or notes from referring doctors.

## 24 Hours Before the Appointment
- [ ] **Top 3 Priorities**: Write down the top 3 questions or symptoms you want to address. Clinicians have limited time; lead with your highest-priority concern first.
- [ ] **Symptom Log**: Note when symptoms began, daily patterns, triggers, severity (1-10 scale), and what treatments or remedies have or have not helped.
- [ ] **Life Changes**: Note any major life events, dietary changes, family health changes, or significant stressors over the past 6 months.

## Day of the Appointment
- [ ] **Arrive Early**: Plan to arrive 15 minutes before scheduled check-in time (or 5 minutes early for telehealth).
- [ ] **Bring a Companion**: If dealing with complex health information, consider bringing a trusted friend or family member to help take notes.
- [ ] **Notepad & Pen**: Have a notebook or digital note ready to record clinician recommendations, follow-up dates, and prescribed instructions.

## During the Appointment
- [ ] State your primary goal or concern in the first 2 minutes of the consultation.
- [ ] Ask for clarification if the clinician uses medical jargon or unfamiliar terms.
- [ ] Repeat key instructions back to the clinician to ensure mutual understanding ("So I should expect...").
- [ ] Clarify next steps: Who orders tests? When should results be expected? When is the follow-up visit?

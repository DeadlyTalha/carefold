# CGM & Glycemic Log Summary Guide

A clinical reference guide to continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) metrics, target ranges, and discussion preparation for diabetes appointments.

## 1. Standard Ambulatory Glucose Profile (AGP) Targets
Established consensus targets for non-pregnant adults with Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes:

| Metric | Clinical Definition | Standard Target | What It Indicates |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time in Range (TIR)** | $70\text{--}180\text{ mg/dL}$ ($3.9\text{--}10.0\text{ mmol/L}$) | $>70\%$ of readings | Primary indicator of glycemic stability and reduced microvascular complication risk |
| **Time Below Range (TBR Level 1)** | $54\text{--}69\text{ mg/dL}$ ($3.0\text{--}3.8\text{ mmol/L}$) | $<4\%$ of readings | Mild-to-moderate hypoglycemia requiring dietary treatment |
| **Time Below Range (TBR Level 2)** | $<54\text{ mg/dL}$ ($<3.0\text{ mmol/L}$) | $<1\%$ of readings | Urgent hypoglycemia; clinical priority to eliminate |
| **Time Above Range (TAR Level 1)** | $181\text{--}250\text{ mg/dL}$ ($10.1\text{--}13.9\text{ mmol/L}$) | $<25\%$ of readings | Postprandial or basal hyperglycemia |
| **Time Above Range (TAR Level 2)** | $>250\text{ mg/dL}$ ($>13.9\text{ mmol/L}$) | $<5\%$ of readings | Severe hyperglycemia; risk factor for dehydration and ketosis |
| **Glucose Variability (%CV)** | Coefficient of Variation ($\text{SD} / \text{Mean} \times 100$) | $\le 36\%$ | Measures the magnitude of swings; $\le 36\%$ indicates stable glucose |

## 2. Glucose Pattern Observation Worksheet
Before meeting your clinician, identify recurring trends rather than isolated numbers:
- **Nocturnal Hypoglycemia**: Do you experience low glucose readings between midnight and 06:00? (May indicate excess basal insulin or late-evening exercise).
- **Morning Dawn Phenomenon**: Does glucose rise sharply between 04:00 and 08:00 before eating?
- **Postprandial Spikes**: Which specific meals or meal times consistently cause glucose to exceed $180\text{ mg/dL}$ two hours post-meal?
- **Exercise-Induced Drops**: Does physical activity cause precipitous drops during or several hours after workouts?

## 3. High-Value CGM Questions for Your Clinician
- Looking at my 14-day or 30-day AGP report, which time of day represents the greatest opportunity to increase my Time in Range?
- Am I experiencing any unrecognized overnight hypoglycemia that we need to address first?
- How should we adjust my management plan to smooth out large glycemic fluctuations?
- Are my CGM alert thresholds (high/low alarms) set appropriately to minimize alarm fatigue while keeping me safe?

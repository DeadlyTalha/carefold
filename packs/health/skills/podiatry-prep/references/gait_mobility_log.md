# Gait & Mobility Log Template

Tracking worksheet for patients with foot pain, balance issues, or mobility changes.

## Purpose
Changes in walking pattern, balance, and mobility are important clues to foot, nerve, or circulation problems. A structured log helps your podiatrist understand your daily experience and track changes over time.

## How to Record
- Note when symptoms occur (morning, after walking, at night).
- Describe the location (heel, ball of foot, toes, arch, ankle).
- Note what makes it better or worse (rest, shoes, ice, elevation).
- Note any near-falls, falls, or need for support (wall, cane, furniture).
- Note the distance or time you can walk before symptoms start.

## Weekly Summary
- Any falls or near-falls this week? Yes / No
- Any change in walking distance? Yes / No
- Any new pain at rest or at night? Yes / No
- Using shoes and orthotics as prescribed? Yes / No

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed 14-day log.
- Your current shoes and orthotics.
- A list of any falls or near-falls, with the circumstances.

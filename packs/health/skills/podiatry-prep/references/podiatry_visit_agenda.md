# Podiatry Visit Agenda Reference

High-yield question banks, discussion checklists, and appointment preparation milestones for podiatry, foot and ankle, and wound care clinic visits.

## Before the Visit: Preparation Checklist
- Collect all recent diagnostic reports: X-rays, MRI, CT, ABI, monofilament testing, and vibration testing results.
- Bring a written list of current medications, including diabetes therapies, anticoagulants, and antibiotics.
- Prepare your diabetic foot check log covering the past 7-14 days, with photos of any wounds.
- Prepare your gait and mobility log, noting balance changes, assistive devices, and pain patterns.
- Bring your current footwear and orthotics, or clear photos of them.
- Write down your top 3-5 concerns and prioritize them.

## High-Yield Questions by Topic

### Diabetic Foot Exam & Prevention
- What is my risk category for diabetic foot ulcers, and how often should I be screened?
- Did my monofilament and vibration testing show any change from last time?
- What daily foot-check routine do you recommend for me?
- Should I see a podiatrist more often than once a year?

### Wounds & Ulcers
- What stage is my wound, and is it healing at the expected rate?
- Do I need offloading, debridement, or a special dressing?
- What signs of infection should make me call you or go to the ER?
- Do I need imaging to rule out bone involvement?

### Neuropathy & Sensation
- What does my sensory testing show, and is it stable?
- What foot care routine do you recommend for neuropathy?
- Are there treatments that could help with my tingling or burning?

### Gait, Balance & Mobility
- What do you see in my walking pattern that I should be aware of?
- Would physical therapy or an assistive device help?
- What exercises can improve my balance and reduce fall risk?

### Orthotics & Footwear
- What type of orthotic do I need, and how should I break it in?
- What shoes do you recommend for my foot shape and condition?
- Do I need custom vs. over-the-counter orthotics?

### Nails, Calluses & Skin
- How should I care for my nails at home safely?
- Should a professional remove my calluses or ingrown nails?
- What moisturizer or skin care routine do you recommend?

### Procedures & Recovery
- What are the risks and expected recovery time for the proposed procedure?
- What medications should I stop before the procedure, and when?
- What warning signs after the procedure should prompt me to call or go to the ER?

## Appointment Milestones
1. **Arrival**: Check in, provide insurance and medication list, and mention any current wounds or anticoagulation.
2. **Pre-visit**: Ask the nurse to remove shoes and socks, and to record any new wounds or skin changes.
3. **Consultation**: Lead with your top concern, then move through your prioritized questions.
4. **Diagnostics**: Ask whether any imaging, ABI, or sensory testing will be done today or scheduled separately.
5. **Wrap-up**: Confirm the follow-up interval, footwear and offloading instructions, and who to call for questions.

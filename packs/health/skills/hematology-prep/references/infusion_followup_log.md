# Infusion Follow-Up Log Template

Tracking worksheet for patients receiving intravenous (IV) iron, B12 injections, or other hematology infusions.

## Purpose
Infusion response and side effects are key to adjusting your treatment plan. A structured log helps your hematologist understand how you tolerate the therapy, how quickly your symptoms improve, and whether the dose or schedule needs to change.

## How to Record
- Note the date and type of infusion (IV iron, B12, other).
- Note how you felt in the 24-48 hours after the infusion.
- Note any side effects: flushing, headache, nausea, muscle pain, rash, dizziness, or fever.
- Note symptom response over the following 1-2 weeks (fatigue, pica, restless legs, dyspnea).
- Note any delayed reactions (up to 1 week after infusion).

## Weekly Summary
- Did symptoms improve after the infusion? Yes / No
- Any side effects that lasted more than 24 hours? Yes / No
- Any new symptoms since the infusion? Yes / No
- Would you rate the infusion as: Very helpful / Helpful / No change / Worse

## What to Bring to Your Visit
- The completed infusion log.
- A list of any side effects, with timing and severity.
- Any lab results drawn after the infusion (if available).

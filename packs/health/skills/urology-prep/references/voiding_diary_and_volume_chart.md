# Voiding Diary & Frequency-Volume Chart

A voiding diary (frequency-volume chart) is a valuable objective clinical tool used by urologists to evaluate lower urinary tract symptoms, urinary frequency, nocturia, and incontinence. This 3-day template helps patients capture accurate data regarding fluid intake and urinary patterns.

## 1. Instructions for Accurate Data Collection
1. **Measurement**: Use a calibrated plastic collection container ("urinal hat" placed in the toilet) to measure voided urine volume in milliliters (mL) or ounces (oz).
2. **Timing**: Record data across 24 to 72 consecutive hours, including nighttime awakenings.
3. **Fluid Intake**: Record the type and exact amount of all fluids consumed (water, coffee, tea, soda, alcohol).
4. **Urgency & Leakage**: Note whether the urge was mild, moderate, or severe, and whether any involuntary leakage occurred.

## 2. 24-Hour Logging Table
- **Urgency Scale**: 0 = None, 1 = Mild (can delay voiding), 2 = Moderate (must void soon), 3 = Severe (overwhelming immediate urge).
- **Leakage Scale**: 0 = None, 1 = Drops, 2 = Wet underwear, 3 = Soaked outer clothing or pad.

## 3. Daily Summary Metrics
Calculate these summary values at the end of each 24-hour recording period:
- **Total Fluid Intake (mL)**: [e.g., 1,350 mL]
- **Total 24-Hour Urine Output (mL)**: [e.g., 1,460 mL]
- **Total Daytime Voids**: [e.g., 6 voids]
- **Total Nighttime Voids (Nocturia)**: [e.g., 2 voids]
- **Maximum Single Voided Volume (mL)**: [e.g., 220 mL]

## 4. Consultation Questions for Your Urologist
1. "Based on my frequency-volume logs, is my total daily urine output and bladder capacity within normal limits?"
2. "Does my nocturia pattern point toward nocturnal polyuria, sleep apnea, or bladder outlet obstruction?"
3. "Would behavioral modifications, such as timing my fluid intake or reducing caffeine in the evening, improve my sleep quality?"

## 5. Emergency Warning Signs
Do not delay medical care. Seek emergency medical attention (911 or emergency department) immediately if you experience acute inability to urinate accompanied by severe abdominal pain (urinary retention), visible gross blood in urine with clots, high fever with severe flank pain, or sudden severe testicular pain.

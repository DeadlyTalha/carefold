# Rash & Flare-up Documentation Protocol

A systematic worksheet for recording rash symptoms, distribution, and potential environmental or product triggers.

## 1. Rash Chronology & Descriptive Log
- **Date of First Appearance**: ____________________
- **Initial Location**: (Where did the rash start? e.g., inner wrists, neck, abdomen): ____________________
- **Pattern of Spread**: (Has it remained localized or spread to other regions?): ____________________
- **Visual Description**:
  - [ ] Flat discolored spots (macules)
  - [ ] Small raised bumps (papules)
  - [ ] Raised, flat-topped plaques (scaly, thickened)
  - [ ] Fluid-filled blisters or tiny vesicles
  - [ ] Pus-filled bumps (pustules)
  - [ ] Raised, smooth hives/welts (wheals)
- **Associated Sensations**:
  - Pruritus (Itching) Level: ____ / 10 (0 = None, 10 = Severe, preventing sleep)
  - Burning or Stinging: (Yes / No)
  - Pain or Tenderness to touch: (Yes / No)

## 2. Potential Trigger Exposure Inventory
Document exposures occurring in the 24 to 72 hours before the rash appeared:
- **Personal Care Products**: New soaps, body washes, moisturizers, deodorants, perfumes, hair dyes, or cosmetics.
- **Laundry & Household**: New detergents, fabric softeners, bleach, or cleaning solvents.
- **Medications & Supplements**: Any new oral prescription medications, over-the-counter pain relievers, antibiotics, or herbal supplements.
- **Occupational & Environmental**: Chemical exposures, latex gloves, metal jewelry (nickel in watchbands, belt buckles), outdoor vegetation (poison ivy/oak), or unusual sun exposure.
- **Dietary**: New foods, seafood, nuts, or preservatives.

## 3. Topical Treatment Adherence Log
List all topical products currently being applied to the rash:
- Product name and strength: ____________________
- Frequency of application (times per day): ____________________
- Vehicle type (ointment, cream, lotion, gel): ____________________
- Response noted (improvement, worsening, stinging upon application): ____________________

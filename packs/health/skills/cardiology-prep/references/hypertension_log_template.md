# 14-Day Home Blood Pressure & Heart Rate Tracking Worksheet

This worksheet provides a standardized format for logging home blood pressure (BP) and pulse readings prior to your cardiology consultation.

## Home Blood Pressure Measurement Best Practices
- **Rest First**: Sit quietly for 5 minutes before taking a reading.
- **Posture**: Keep your back supported, feet flat on the floor, and arm supported at heart level.
- **Timing**: Take two readings in the morning (before morning medications and breakfast) and two readings in the evening (before dinner or bed).
- **Avoid Prior to Testing**: Avoid caffeine, exercise, and tobacco for at least 30 minutes before measurement.

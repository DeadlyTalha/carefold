# 30-Day Migraine & Headache Tracking Diary

Headache specialists emphasize that an accurate diary is the most critical diagnostic tool for distinguishing episodic migraine, chronic migraine, tension-type headache, and medication-overuse headache.

## Key Information to Log for Each Headache Episode
- **Onset & Duration**: Exact time headache started, peak severity time, and total hours until resolution.
- **Pain Severity**: Scale of 0 (No pain) to 10 (Incapacitating, cannot perform daily tasks).
- **Pain Quality & Location**: Throbbing/pulsating, dull pressure, stabbing; unilateral (one-sided) or bilateral (both sides).
- **Associated Symptoms**: Nausea, vomiting, sensitivity to light (photophobia), sensitivity to sound (phonophobia), dizziness.
- **Aura Phenomena**: Flashing zigzag lines (scintillating scotoma), blind spots, tingling in fingers or face, speech hesitation.
- **Medications Taken & Response**: Exact medication taken, dose, time taken, and pain score 2 hours after ingestion.

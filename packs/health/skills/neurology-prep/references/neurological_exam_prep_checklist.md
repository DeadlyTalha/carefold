# Neurological Exam Preparation & Consultation Checklist

Preparing for a comprehensive neurological examination and consultation.

## 1. What to Expect During the Physical Neurological Exam
The neurologist will evaluate several primary systems:
- **Cranial Nerves**: Testing eye tracking, facial symmetry, tongue movement, hearing, and pupillary reflexes.
- **Motor Examination**: Checking muscle tone, strength in arms and legs, and observing for resting or action tremors.
- **Deep Tendon Reflexes**: Testing biceps, triceps, patellar (knee), and Achilles reflexes using a reflex hammer.
- **Sensory Examination**: Assessing sensitivity to light touch, pinprick, temperature, and vibration sensation in feet and hands.
- **Coordination & Gait**: Observing walking balance, tandem gait (heel-to-toe walking), and finger-to-nose coordination.

## 2. Records and Items to Bring
- [ ] List of all current prescription and OTC medications, including vitamins (especially Vitamin B6 and B12).
- [ ] Imaging discs (CD/DVD) and radiologist narrative reports for all brain and spine MRI or CT scans.
- [ ] Reports of previous EMGs, nerve conduction velocity (NCV) tests, or EEG recordings.
- [ ] Footwear that is easy to remove for sensory and reflex testing.

## 3. High-Yield Questions to Ask Your Neurologist
- Based on my symptoms and physical examination, where in the nervous system (brain, spinal cord, peripheral nerves, muscles) does this issue appear to originate?
- Are further diagnostic studies (such as high-resolution MRI, lumbar puncture, or blood autoantibody panels) recommended?
- Is my current headache frequency classified as episodic or chronic, and am I a candidate for daily preventive therapy?
- Are there non-pharmacological or lifestyle modifications (sleep consistency, hydration, stress management) that have proven efficacy for my condition?

A structured functional evaluation guide to quantify joint symptoms and daily living impact before meeting an orthopedic specialist.

*Note: Morning joint stiffness lasting $>30\text{--}60$ minutes may suggest inflammatory arthritis, whereas brief stiffness (<15 minutes) is typical of mechanical osteoarthritis.*

## 2. Functional Activities of Daily Living (ADL) Impact Checklist
Orthopedic surgeons prioritize functional deficits when evaluating conservative versus surgical options:
- **Ambulation / Walking**:
  - [ ] Unlimited walking distance without pain
  - [ ] Can walk 4–6 blocks before stopping due to joint pain
  - [ ] Limited to 1–2 blocks
  - [ ] Housebound or requiring assistive device (cane, walker, crutches)
- **Stair Navigation**:
  - [ ] Ascends/descends stairs normally (foot-over-foot)
  - [ ] Ascends normally, but descends one step at a time
  - [ ] Requires handrail and takes one step at a time for both ascending and descending
  - [ ] Cannot navigate stairs
- **Transfers (Sitting & Rising)**:
  - [ ] Rises from chair without using armrests
  - [ ] Requires pushing off armrests with arms
  - [ ] Requires multiple attempts or physical assistance
- **Personal Care**:
  - [ ] Putting on socks and shoes without difficulty
  - [ ] Getting in and out of the shower / bathtub safely
  - [ ] Bending over to pick up objects from the floor

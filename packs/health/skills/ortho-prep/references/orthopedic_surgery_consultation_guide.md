# Orthopedic Surgery Consultation Guide

A guide for preparing comprehensive, informed discussions with an orthopedic surgeon regarding joint replacement or corrective procedures.

## 1. Core Consultation Questions
### About the Surgical Recommendation
- Based on my physical exam and imaging, what specific structural damage is present in my joint?
- Am I an immediate candidate for surgery, or are there further conservative options (injections, specialized physical therapy) we should exhaust first?
- What are the risks of postponing surgery for 6 to 12 months?

### About the Surgical Approach & Implant
- What surgical technique or approach do you recommend (e.g., anterior vs. posterior hip approach; robotic-assisted vs. manual knee replacement)?
- What type of implant will be used, what is its expected longevity, and what materials is it made of?
- How many of these specific procedures do you perform each year?

### About the Hospital Stay & Recovery
- Is this procedure performed as an outpatient (same-day discharge) or will it require an overnight hospital stay?
- What will my weight-bearing status be immediately following surgery?
- What is the anticipated timeline for:
  - Walking unassisted without a walker or cane?
  - Driving a car?
  - Returning to desk work vs. physically demanding activities?
- What specific physical therapy protocol will I follow post-operatively?

### About Pain Management & Complication Prevention
- What multi-modal pain management protocol do you utilize to control post-operative pain while minimizing opioid use?
- What blood clot (DVT/PE) prevention regimen will I be placed on (aspirin, anticoagulants, compression boots)?
- What is your post-operative infection prevention protocol?

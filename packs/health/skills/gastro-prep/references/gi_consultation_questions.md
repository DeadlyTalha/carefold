# Gastroenterology Consultation Questions Guide

A categorized question bank for visits concerning chronic GI symptoms, GERD, and screening.

## 1. Questions Regarding Persistent Acid Reflux / GERD
- Do my symptoms suggest uncomplicated GERD, or should we evaluate for erosive esophagitis or Barrett's esophagus with an upper endoscopy?
- What are the long-term benefits and risks of daily proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) versus H2 blockers?
- What sleeping and dietary lifestyle habits (e.g. elevating the head of the bed, meal timing before sleep) provide the greatest benefit?

## 2. Questions Regarding Chronic Abdominal Pain, Bloating & Altered Bowel Habits (IBS/IBD)
- What diagnostic tests (e.g. fecal calprotectin, celiac serology, thyroid panel, stool pathogens) are appropriate to rule out inflammatory or infectious causes?
- Would a clinically supervised trial of the Low FODMAP diet be appropriate for my symptoms, and can you refer me to a registered GI dietitian?
- Are there specific antispasmodics, bile acid binders, or gut-directed therapies suited to my predominant bowel pattern?

## 3. Questions Following a Colonoscopy or Endoscopy
- Were any polyps detected and removed during my procedure, and what was their histological classification (e.g. tubular adenoma, hyperplastic)?
- Based on the number and pathology of the polyps removed, what is my recommended surveillance colonoscopy interval (e.g. 3, 5, or 10 years)?
- Were any tissue biopsies taken to check for H. pylori, microscopic colitis, or celiac disease?

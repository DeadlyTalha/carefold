# Nephrology Appointment Agenda & Preparation Checklist

Maximize your consultation time by bringing organized medical records, medication lists, and prioritized questions.

## 1. Materials to Bring to the Clinic
- [ ] Complete medication list with exact strengths, dosing frequency, and start dates.
- [ ] Over-the-counter list: Pain relievers, vitamins, herbal remedies, and protein powders.
- [ ] Recent laboratory reports from primary care or other specialists (within past 3–6 months).
- [ ] 14-day home blood pressure log (morning and evening readings).
- [ ] 7-day fluid and weight log.
- [ ] Copy of recent kidney ultrasound, CT scan, or previous biopsy reports if performed.

## 2. Priority Consultation Questions
### Understanding Kidney Status & Disease Trajectory
- What is my current estimated kidney function, and has it changed significantly since my last visit?
- What specific condition is damaging my kidneys?
- What is our primary therapeutic goal over the next 6 to 12 months?

### Medications & Safe Practices
- Are all my current medications safe for my kidneys at my current filtration level?
- Which pain medications are safe for me to take for occasional headaches or joint pain?
- If I ever need an imaging scan with IV contrast dye, what precautions should my doctors take?

### Diet, Lifestyle & Monitoring
- What are my personalized daily limits for sodium, potassium, and phosphorus?
- Should I be limiting my daily protein intake to a specific number of grams per kilogram?
- How often do I need follow-up blood and urine tests?
